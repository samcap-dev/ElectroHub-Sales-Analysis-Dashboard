# 📊 ElectroHub Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This Power BI project focuses on analyzing the sales performance of **ElectroHub**, a retail company selling products across categories such as **Footwear, Clothes, Home Appliances, Accessories, Kitchenware, Toys, and Personal Care**.  
The dashboard provides **interactive views of sales, profits, and discounts** over multiple time periods, enabling management to take **data-driven business decisions**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Monitor overall **sales and profitability** across different product categories.
- Identify **top-selling products** and products with heavy discounts.
- Understand the impact of **discounts on profitability**.
- Compare sales trends across **daily, monthly, quarterly, and yearly** timelines.
- Enable **management and sales teams** to filter and drill down into specific categories or products.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features
- 📌 **Sales & Profit Analysis** → Revenue, profit margin, and growth trends.  
- 🏆 **Top-Selling Products** → Identifies best-performing items by sales volume.  
- 💸 **Discount Insights** → Highlights products and categories most affected by discounting.  
- 📊 **Category-Wise Breakdown** → Sales distribution across categories.  
- 📅 **Time-Based Comparison** → Daily, monthly, quarterly, and yearly performance tracking.  
- 🎛️ **Interactive Filters** → Drill down by product, category, and date.  

---

## 🛠️ Skills & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop** → Dashboard building and visualization.  
- **Power Query** → Cleaning and transforming raw sales data.  
- **DAX** → Creating custom measures (Profit %, YoY Growth, Discount Impact).  
- **Data Modeling** → Establishing relationships between sales, product, and time data.  
- **Visualization Techniques** → Slicers, bar/line charts, and KPI indicators.

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Download the `.pbix` file.  
2. Open it in **Power BI Desktop**.  
3. Use slicers to filter sales and profit by category or time period.  


---

## 📢 Key Insights
- **Footwear and Home Appliances** generated the highest revenue.  
- Discounts boosted **short-term sales** but negatively affected profit margins.  
- Sales spiked significantly in **Q4 (festive season)**.  
- **Toys and Accessories underperformed**, suggesting the need for new marketing strategies.
